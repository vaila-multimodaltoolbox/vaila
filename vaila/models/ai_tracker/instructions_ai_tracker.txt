# AI Tracker Discriminator Checkpoints & Weights
This directory is the sole canonical local location for the AI Tracker (getpixelvideo.py):
online-discriminator checkpoints (*.npz) and backbone weights (resnet50_imagenet.pth, and
optionally resnet152_imagenet.pth, mobilenet_v3_small_imagenet.pth, efficientnet_b0_imagenet.pth).
There is no other fallback directory under vaila/models/.
Checkpoints are incrementally updated across sessions (JIT learning / transfer learning) and are
kept local to the user/machine. resnet152_imagenet.pth, mobilenet_v3_small_imagenet.pth, and
efficientnet_b0_imagenet.pth are all optional (opt-in variants, selectable as primary_variant/
resnet_variant or as the cascade fallback_variant); resnet50_imagenet.pth (or the Torch hub
cache) is used by default.
Do not commit large weights or user-specific .npz models to git.
