Time-varying Vitruvian DLT3D

Module: vaila/soccerfield_vitruvian_dlt3d.py
Version: 0.3.104 · 11 August 2026
Role: pitch plane + player/goal vertical controls → one DLT3D row per supported frame

Geometry

Pitch landmarks determine the eight projective coefficients visible on Z=0. The bottom centre of a tracked player bbox is inverted through that plane to (X,Y,0). Its top centre is assigned (X,Y,h) from an anthropometric height prior. These non-coplanar controls estimate the remaining DLT coefficients that multiply Z.

Measured goalpost or scene verticals may be added with a higher weight. Player boxes are the Vitruvian fallback when those structures are not visible.

Rank and interpretation: one vertical gives only two equations. At least two spatially distinct verticals and rank three are required. This calibrates a camera; it does not turn arbitrary single-view joint observations into unique 3D points.

Inputs

--field-pixelsPer-frame 32-pitch-point CSV
--bbox-bottomSAM bottom-centre anchors

Example

uv run python -m vaila.soccerfield_vitruvian_dlt3d \
  --field-pixels runs/pitch/field_keypoints_getpixelvideo.csv \
  --bbox-bottom runs/sam/VIDEO/sam_vaila_bottom.csv \
  --bbox-top runs/sam/VIDEO/sam_vaila_top.csv \
  --heights anthropometry_match.csv \
  --output runs/vitruvian_dlt3d

Outputs and cautions

The tool writes a multirow .dlt3d, per-frame diagnostics, the complete vertical control table, and height-assignment provenance. Bbox controls are down-weighted because pose, jumps, occlusion, and mask padding perturb the apparent vertical. Frames that do not satisfy field-count or rank requirements are reported and skipped. Raw DLT coefficients are not interpolated.
